# Compact Cross-Mode Example

This example demonstrates how outputs relate. It is not a required genre or plot formula.

## Project excerpt

- **Title:** 《旧物回声》
- **Premise:** 28岁的文物修复师林澈能听见古物最后一位持有者留下的记忆，却会逐渐混淆他人的记忆与自己的人生。
- **Audience promise:** 每件古物开启一个可视化谜团，同时推进一桩贯穿全季的失踪案。
- **Visual identity:** 铜绿、修复金线、记忆中的暖红逆光。
- **Configured scope:** 80 episodes, 120 seconds, 5 per batch.

## Outline excerpt

| Act | Episodes | Change |
|---|---:|---|
| Trigger | 1–16 | 林澈第一次用古物记忆救人，并发现失踪姐姐的声音 |
| Expansion | 17–32 | 他与31岁的调查记者周遥合作，追踪被人为拆散的一组祭器 |
| Reversal | 33–48 | 记忆证明姐姐曾主动加入对方，但证据可能被伪造 |
| Breakdown | 49–64 | 能力失控，林澈不再相信自己的童年记忆，搭档关系破裂 |
| Finale | 65–80 | 两人用可验证的现实证据重建真相，决定哪些记忆应被公开 |

## Character excerpt

**林澈，28岁**

- Goal: 找到姐姐并证明自己的记忆仍可信。
- Agency: 决定是否触碰古物、公开谁的记忆，以及承受何种代价。
- Identity anchors: 清瘦成年男性；短黑卷发；左眉细疤；深灰工装围裙；铜柄放大镜；观察物体时习惯屏住呼吸。
- Ability limit: 每次读取只出现最后90秒记忆；连续使用会把第一人称记忆错认成自身经历。

**周遥，31岁**

- Goal: 查清父亲被栽赃的旧案，不接受只靠超能力得出的结论。
- Agency: 建立证据链、拒绝发布不可验证的记忆，并在中点主动离开合作。
- Identity anchors: 成年女性；齐肩直发；右耳银色耳夹；沙色短风衣；红边采访本；质疑时会转动录音笔。

## Review excerpt

**Required finding — mechanism continuity**

- Evidence: Episode 011, shot 4.
- Expectation: `characters.md` states the ability shows the final 90 seconds from the last holder's viewpoint.
- Problem: the shot currently presents an external view of the sister without establishing whose viewpoint it is.
- Consequence: the ability behaves like an unrestricted flashback and loses its investigative limit.
- Revision: place a reflected hand holding the mirror in foreground and identify that holder in the next shot; keep all other beats unchanged.

**Required finding — repeated hook function**

- Evidence: Episode 010 ends by revealing the sister in a reflection; Episode 011, shot 4 repeats the same revelation without changing its meaning.
- Expectation: adjacent endings and payoffs should not reuse the same hook family unless the second beat transforms the first.
- Consequence: Episode 011 feels like a replay rather than new evidence.
- Revision: retain the sister image as the payoff, then end Episode 011 on a dilemma—publishing the verified frame will expose the witness who supplied the mirror.
